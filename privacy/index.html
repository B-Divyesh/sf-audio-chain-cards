Plain-language policy

Privacy

Effective 28 August 2026

Your work stays local

Chain Cards stores cards, steps, timestamp labels, and completion history in your browser’s IndexedDB storage. It does not send this information to us. Audio you choose is opened through a temporary local browser URL: it is not uploaded, copied into the card database, or retained after you detach it or close the tab.

What we collect

The shipped app has no account, analytics, advertising, tracking pixel, or third-party runtime script. A static hosting provider may keep ordinary security and access logs, such as IP address, requested path, and user agent, according to its own retention policy.

Your controls

Export a card as JSON whenever you want a portable copy. Delete a card inside its workbench, or clear this site’s storage in browser settings to remove every local card. Deleting browser data cannot remove JSON files you previously exported.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the project’s public source repository. Do not include private recordings or sensitive file paths.
