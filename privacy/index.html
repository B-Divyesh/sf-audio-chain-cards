Privacy policy

How Chain Cards handles your data

Effective 5 September 2026

Your cards stay in this browser

Cards, steps, labels, and history are stored in this browser’s IndexedDB.

We do not receive this data.

Selected audio uses a temporary browser address. It is not uploaded or stored in a card.

The demo stays separate

The demo uses session storage with keys that start with demo:.

Demo changes never enter your saved card database. Leaving or resetting the demo removes those changes.

What the app collects

The app has no accounts, analytics, ads, tracking pixels, or third-party runtime scripts.

Our static host may keep security logs such as an IP address, path, and browser type.

Export or remove your cards

Export a card as JSON when you need a portable copy.

Delete one card in its workbench. Clear this site’s browser storage to remove every saved card.

Privacy questions

Open an issue in the public source repository. Do not include recordings or private file paths.
